1. Executive Summary & Program Context

The Government of Telangana, in collaboration with AIKAM and the Department of Energy, has launched the EnergyTech Innovation Challenge to deploy transformative artificial intelligence across the state’s electricity distribution utilities: TGSPDCL (Telangana Southern Power Distribution Company Limited) and TGNPDCL (Telangana Northern Power Distribution Company Limited).

Across more than 1.75 Crore (17.5 Million) registered electricity connections in Telangana, billing inquiries, tariff slab confusion, Gruha Jyothi subsidy status questions, and meter reading disputes account for over 68% of all inbound citizen calls to the 1912 utility hotline and section customer care offices.

The UrjaMitra AI Mission
UrjaMitra AI is an autonomous, multi-agent conversational and billing copilot engineered specifically for Telangana power utilities. It bridges the communication gap between complex regulatory tariff orders (TGERC slabs, Gruha Jyothi zero-bill eligibility, fuel surcharge adjustments) and citizens across urban, peri-urban, and rural Telangana via WhatsApp, Web, and Voice in Telugu, Hindi, and English.

3. Problem Statement & Root Cause Analysis

1. Telescopic Slab Transitions & Citizen Bill Shock

Telangana domestic LT Category-I billing operates under telescopic slab rates with consumption brackets: Band A (0–100 kWh), Band B (101–200 kWh), and Band C (>200 kWh). Crossing even 1 single unit from Band B (199 units) to Band C (201 units) transitions the entire base energy calculation into higher per-unit rates, accompanied by sharp escalation in monthly customer charges. Citizens perceive this as an arbitrary meter jump.

2. Gruha Jyothi 200-Unit Cliff Edge

Under the flagship Gruha Jyothi Scheme, eligible domestic consumers consuming ≤ 200 units receive a Zero Bill (100% state subsidy). If consumption hits 201 units, the subsidy is nullified or significantly curtailed under government rules, triggering a sudden ₹1,000+ bill. Citizens flood MeeSeva centers and DISCOM offices seeking answers.

3. Door Lock (Status 02) & Average Billing Accumulations

When physical meter readers encounter locked gates or damaged optical sensors, the utility billing engine records "Average Billing" (Status 02). In subsequent months, when an actual meter reading is performed, the accumulated multi-month consumption pushes the consumer into the highest tariff tier (₹10–11/unit), creating severe dispute escalations.

6. Functional Requirements Breakdown

FR-1: Telescopic Slab Deconstruction Engine
Ingests Unique Service Number (USC No) or bill amount and itemizes Energy Charges per bracket (0–100, 101–200, 201–300, 301–400, >400 units), Fixed Charges, Customer Charges, and Electricity Duty. For Gruha Jyothi consumers, clearly highlights zero-bill subsidy grant and exact units remaining before crossing the 200-unit threshold.
FR-2: Multimodal Bill & Meter Vision OCR
Accepts physical paper bill photos, PDFs, and live meter display photos. Extracts USC No, Billing Date, Previous Reading, Present Reading, and Status (01 Normal, 02 Door Lock). Automatically compares live consumer meter photos against billed values to identify Average Billing errors.
FR-3: Multilingual Vernacular Voice & Chat Copilot
Native support in Telugu (తెలుగు), Hindi (हिन्दी), and English. Handles regional accents and mixed Telugu-English phrasing. Delivers conversational responses via Web, WhatsApp Business Cloud API, and 1912 IVR voice bridge.
FR-4: Proactive Consumption & Spike Advisory
Clusters consumption into Time-of-Day (ToD) buckets and alerts consumers before they breach subsidy slabs (e.g., "You used 188 units with 4 days remaining. Staying under 200 units saves ₹1,200 under Gruha Jyothi"). Includes PM Surya Ghar rooftop solar ROI calculations.
FR-5: Automated Dispute Resolution & SAP Ticket Agent
Calculates provisional revised bills for verified anomalies, packages cryptographic evidence (image hash, OCR confidence, timestamp), and posts directly into the TGSPDCL/TGNPDCL SAP IS-U complaint queue with human-in-the-loop Section Officer approval.

7. Regulatory Compliance & DPDP Act 2023

TGERC Retail Supply Tariff Alignment: All calculation engines are bound strictly to the latest Telangana State Electricity Regulatory Commission (TGERC) Gazette notifications. No LLM hallucinations are permitted in monetary amounts.

Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023: Full adherence to citizen privacy laws. Consumer identifiers, phone numbers, and bank details are encrypted using AES-256 at rest and masked in analytical logs. Compute and storage reside exclusively in MeitY-empanelled data centers in India.
