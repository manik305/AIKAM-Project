1. Executive Summary & Strategic Positioning

The AIKAM EnergyTech Innovation Challenge brings together the Government of Telangana, the Department of Energy, and state utilities TGSPDCL and TGNPDCL. The competition evaluates technology solutions capable of solving immediate operational bottlenecks across 1.75 Crore power connections in Telangana.

The Three Winning Pillars of UrjaMitra AI
1. Zero-Hallucination Architecture: Absolute guarantee of monetary accuracy. LLMs handle dialogue; a deterministic Python engine calculates official TGERC slab rates.
2. Telugu-First Vernacular Inclusion: True voice and chat accessibility in Telangana Telugu dialects for rural, urban, and agricultural consumers.
3. Closed-Loop Enterprise Resolution: Solves disputes on the spot by generating pre-validated SAP IS-U tickets with meter photo evidence, eliminating paper files.
