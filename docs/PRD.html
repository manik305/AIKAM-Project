1. Product Overview & Architectural Axiom

UrjaMitra AI is an enterprise-grade, distributed multi-agent conversational copilot engineered for Telangana power utilities (TGSPDCL and TGNPDCL). It automates personalized electricity bill explanation, decodes telescopic tariff brackets, verifies meter reading photos, and provides seamless dispute resolution in Telugu, Hindi, and English.

Architectural Axiom: Zero-Hallucination Deterministic Engine
In government utility billing, LLM hallucination on monetary sums is strictly intolerable. UrjaMitra AI enforces a strict separation: Natural Language Understanding is performed by LLM agents via LangGraph; whereas Billing Arithmetic & Tariff Calculation is executed exclusively by a 100% deterministic Python TGERC Tariff Engine implementing gazette formulas.

4. Detailed Agent Specifications (LangGraph)

Agent 1: Telescopic Slab Deconstructor

Decomposes confusing monthly bills into itemized brackets (0–100, 101–200, 201–300, 301–400, >400 units). Resolves the Gruha Jyothi subsidy calculation: if units ≤ 200, applies zero-bill credit; if units > 200, explains the exact rupee variance factors in plain Telugu and English.

Agent 2: Multimodal Bill OCR & Meter Reading Audit

Ingests mobile camera photos of paper bills and live meter LCD screens. Applies perspective correction, extracts USC Number, Previous KWh, Present KWh, and Status (01 Normal, 02 Door Lock). Automatically calculates variance: Calculated Units = (Present - Previous) × MF. If live meter photo shows reading lower than billed reading, flags Average Billing discrepancy.

Agent 3: Proactive Consumption & Spike Advisory

Segments consumption by Time-of-Day (Morning Peak, Solar Hours, Evening Peak, Night Baseload). Detects heatwave cooling spikes and calculates subsidy proximity: Delta = 200 - Projected Units. Provides actionable tips in local vernacular and computes rooftop solar feasibility under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana.

Agent 4: Autonomous Dispute Resolution & SAP Ticket Agent

Assembles cryptographic dispute evidence (image hash, OCR confidence, timestamp, difference delta). Automatically generates a Provisional Revised Bill according to TGERC guidelines and dispatches a structured JSON payload to the DISCOM SAP IS-U BAPI complaint queue with human-in-the-loop Section Officer sign-off.
